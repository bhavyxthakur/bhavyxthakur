### About

I build machine learning systems end to end: from data and model training to the APIs and backends that put models in front of real users.

My strength is the space between research and production. I'm comfortable training and evaluating models in PyTorch or TensorFlow, and equally comfortable wrapping them in clean, well-structured services with FastAPI, proper authentication, and reliable data storage. I care about clear architecture, reproducible experiments, and systems that keep working after the demo.

**What I work on**

- **LLMs & Generative AI**: retrieval-augmented generation, agents and tool use, prompt engineering, working with Hugging Face models
- **Computer Vision**: image processing and camera pipelines with OpenCV, CNN-based detection and classification
- **Deep Learning**: building, training, and fine-tuning neural networks in PyTorch and TensorFlow/Keras
- **Classical ML & Data**: feature engineering, model selection, and evaluation with scikit-learn, pandas, and NumPy
- **ML in production**: serving models behind FastAPI services, containerizing with Docker, designing the backend around them
